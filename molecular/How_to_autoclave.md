# How to autoclave using the LSB 3rd floor autoclave
Created by Emily Humphreys on 9/9/26


1. All people who autoclave should be trained. Alex Hansen leads these trainings about once a quarter.
2. Book a reservation for the 3rd floor autoclave. Do this by going to Biology Intranet → Facilities → Room & equipment reservations. Then, change the drop down to "3rd Floor - Autoclave."
3. Make sure the material you intend to autoclave is autoclave safe and place it in an autoclave bin. Things that are open to the air should get tinfoil on top. The lid on any container with liquid should be loose enough to allow steam to escape. Place a piece of autoclave tape on each item. 
4. Bring your bin to LSB 330, and fill out the autoclave logbook.
5. Put your autoclave bin on the autoclave loading cart. (This cart may be HOT! Use heat resistant gloves.)
6. Unlatch the pull-out tray using the central nob.
7. Slide the pullout tray to load your autoclave bin into the autoclave machine.
8. Unlatch the autoclave loading cart using the side nob.
9. Close the autoclave door using the foot pedal.
10. Select your cycle on touchscreen.
    - Use Dry Cycle 2 for glassware, steel balls, and tubes.
    - Use a wet cycle if you are autoclaving something wet like media or biological material. 
11. Press your selected cycle again to start the autoclave run.
12. Wait for the run to finish. It may take a few minutes more than it says it will.
13. Unload your autoclave bin using the autoclave loading cart. Anything that was in the autoclave will be **HOT!** Use heat resistant gloves.
14. Finish filling out the autoclave log with the max temperature and pressure. You can get these from the paper printout on the autoclave machine.
